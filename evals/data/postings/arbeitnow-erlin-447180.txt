Senior Program Manager at INCO GROUP

About us
INCO is a leading social enterprise on a mission to shape a more inclusive and sustainable economy that creates opportunities for all. We have teams and programs in over 50 countries. Our work spans five complementary activities:
- INCO Academy: skilling programs for people facing barriers to employment, leading to careers in fast-growing industries such as technology, sustainability and healthcare (400,000+ job seekers trained to date).

- INCO Kids: education and awareness programs giving school-age learners future-proof knowledge in technology, sustainability, citizenship and more (350,000+ learners supported to date).

- INCO Entrepreneurs: incubation and acceleration for underrepresented founders and social entrepreneurs (2,000+ businesses supported every year).

- INCO Ventures: impact investing and funding for social and environmental impact startups (€500M+ assets under management).

- INCO Spaces: co-working and co-living spaces for social and environmental innovators (Paris, Berlin, Warsaw and more).

About the program:
AI and the Future of Work is a new three-year research and policy-engagement program (2026–2029), funded through a research grant partnership and led end to end by INCO. It tracks AI's real-world impact on jobs, tasks and productivity across all 27 EU member states, and gives policymakers the evidence, trend baselines and scenario-planning tools to capture AI's productivity upside while managing the workforce transition.

About the role:
We are looking for a Senior Program Manager to run the AI and the Future of Work program day to day and make sure it delivers on time, on budget and to the grant's commitments. You will closely lead and collaborate with a Research Lead and a Policy Lead. 
You will turn a three-year grant into a working operation: a deliverables calendar across 27 countries, a consortium of up to 15 partners, a publication schedule and a reporting cycle the funder can trust. You need to be comfortable working alongside researchers and policy specialists, and fluent in the rhythm of grant-funded research programs.

Responsibilities:
Lead program delivery end to end 
Planning and implementation
- Build and own the three-year implementation plan: workstreams, milestones, dependencies and the deliverables calendar agreed with the funder.

- Run the program's operating rhythm: weekly core-team check-ins, monthly consortium updates and quarterly steering reviews.

- Coordinate the publication pipeline with the Research and Policy Leads: quarterly data refreshes, five semi-annual reports, policy briefs, the annual flagship and the 2029 EU27 report.

- Keep thorough program documentation: plans, decision logs, risk register and handover notes.

- Set up quality-assurance steps for outputs (review cycles, sign-off, editorial and brand checks) together with the Communications team.

- Run the semi-annual tracking cycle and the reporting calendar for the five P0 markets and five sub-regional clusters, so national and EU27 outputs land together.

Monitoring and evaluation
- Define and track program KPIs and grant deliverables; spot slippage early and agree corrective action with the leads.

- Collect and compile performance data across workstreams and partners, and report it to your manager and the funder in a structured, actionable way.

- Coordinate the program's evaluation framework so the team can show what the evidence changed in policy and practice.

Budget and resources
- Own the program budget and cost sheet, including consortium partner budgets and the research consolidation budget held by the Research Lead.

- Manage timelines and scope so the program delivers within the grant's budget and rules; prepare budget forecasts and reallocation requests.

- Ensure grant compliance: eligible costs, audit trail, data protection (GDPR) and the funder's reporting requirements, working with Finance and Legal.

Manage the consortium and partners 
- Run the operational side of partner onboarding with the Research Lead: due diligence, contracts, scopes of work, payment schedules and data-sharing agreements.

- Track the delivery of 8 to 15 national partners against their contracts, and manage underperformance or replacement when needed.

- Organise consortium working groups, review cycles and annual convenings, and keep communication flowing across countries and time zones.

- Run the secretariat for the sub-recipient advisory committee (INCO staff, funder representatives and independent experts): schedule meetings, prepare selection materials, record recommendations and keep the process transparent, with final selection resting with INCO.

- Manage subrecipient compliance: flow-down of grant terms, financial and narrative reporting from partners, and checks before each payment.

Manage the funder and stakeholders
- Act as the primary operational liaison with the grant funder, keeping expectations aligned and surfacing risks early.

- Prepare and present progress, financial and impact reports to the funder and other key stakeholders.

- Work with the Head of Partnerships on funder conversations, renewals and any extension of the partnership.

- Support the Policy Lead and with the logistics of policymaker convenings, events and launches.

- Coordinate, with the Policy Lead, the expanded public-engagement activity in Poland and the CEE region, and the logistics of rapid, fact-based responses when AI labour market stories break.

Coordinate the team
- Set clear responsibilities, timelines and objectives across the core team, and track progress against them.

- Act as the connecting point between the Research Lead, the Policy Lead, data engineers and INCO's support teams (Communications, Finance, Legal, HR).

- Identify staffing needs as the program grows (for example research assistants, data roles or a program officer), write job descriptions, interview and onboard new team members, and manage any program staff assigned to you.

Support growth 
- Work with leadership to standardise how INCO runs grant-funded research programs, so lessons from this one carry over.

- Identify follow-on funding and adjacent opportunities, for example linking findings to INCO Academy skilling programs.

- Build relationships across the European research and policy ecosystem.
