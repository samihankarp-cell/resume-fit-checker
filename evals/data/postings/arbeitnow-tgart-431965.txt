Job description
Marketing is already Flip AI's strongest pipeline channel. Now we want inbound to become our fastest-growing source of new business. We're looking for a VP Marketing who brings the pace of e-commerce and high-growth B2C to a B2B funnel.
You own demand generation across the full funnel: getting the right people's attention, growing traffic and visibility, and turning a first impression into a booked demo. You lead our Marketing team of around 10 people across growth marketing and events, product marketing and brand, and content and SEO/GEO.
You report to our SVP Strategy & Growth, who owns strategy, the budget frame and the board narrative. You own the team, the operating rhythm and the numbers. This is a hands-on leadership role, whether you come from e-commerce, high-ticket DTC, SME SaaS or a B2B scale-up. 

What you can expect
Attention and traffic
- Grow traffic and visibility across paid (search, LinkedIn, ABM), SEO and GEO, so Flip AI shows up wherever buyers search, including AI assistants

- Turn our brand and content into reach that converts, together with our Product Marketing team

- Run a fast creative and channel testing rhythm, steered on cost per outcome

From visitor to booked demo
- Own website conversion end to end: landing pages, demo flow, interactive product tours, testing roadmap and tracking

- Make sure every booked demo is the right one: qualification, routing, speed-to-lead and show rate, together with Sales and RevOps

- Run one weekly funnel review built on SAO and cost per SAO, not MQLs

Team and operating rhythm
- Lead our Head of Growth Marketing, Head of Product Marketing and senior content lead, and build out the digital team

- Make sure brand, social and events feed pipeline: you set how the work is measured, the team brings the craft

- Run a two-week sprint cadence and a weekly performance rhythm that work without you in every meeting

- Support the marketing for our US launch and prepare the funnel for product-led elements
