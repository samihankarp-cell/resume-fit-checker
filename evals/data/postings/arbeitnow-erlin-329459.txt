Full Stack Flutter Engineer – PHP/Go (m/f/x) at SIDES (SimplyDelivery GmbH)

Your mission

About SIDES

Restaurants are where people come together over good food. We build the technology that keeps them running.

SIDES is the operating system for hospitality: POS, payments, delivery, kitchen tools, self-order terminals, apps and webshops on one platform. Thousands of restaurants run on it and millions of transactions flow through it every month, peaking on a Friday night when nothing is allowed to fail.

We have the owner's and the chef's back, so they can focus on their food and their guests. And it is not just code on a screen: our software runs on real hardware in real restaurants, from POS devices to self-order terminals and more.

We love AI. These are wild times for software engineers, and we want to make the most of them: AI is part of every workflow at SIDES, and we are looking for people who are as excited about that as we are. Everyone on the team uses tools like Claude every day to write and review code, explore unfamiliar parts of the codebase, write tests and get from idea to shipped feature faster. We see it as a way to spend less time on boilerplate and more on what matters: good architecture, sound decisions and software that holds up on a busy Friday night. Nobody has figured all of this out yet. We experiment, share what works, drop what doesn't, and keep raising the bar together.

Our tech stack: Flutter (mobile & web), Go, PHP, and AWS
