Senior Software Engineer at Skalar

The opportunity
How taxation, accounting and payroll work today is a pain for everyone. Still every business must do it continuously. It is the backbone our economy runs on.
Enabled through AI, we are turning this often-painful process into a radically simple experience for our customers, so they can focus on what matters to them: growing their business.
The role
As a Senior Software Engineer, you own a product area – e.g. bookkeeping, payroll or client onboarding – and take it from problem to production. You work directly with the founders, product and our tax experts, and you decide how things get built in your area.
We are past the founding phase but still very early: most of what you touch is greenfield.
What you’ll own
- Your product area, end-to-end: Turn customer and tax-expert problems into shipped features (spec → ship → measure).
- APIs, data models and services: Design and build the backend your area runs on – and ship the UI on top.
- AI that is correct, not just plausible: Build AI-powered workflows with evaluation, fallbacks and human review where it matters.
- Quality and operations: Tests, monitoring and incident response for what you ship.
- Raising the bar: Code reviews, mentoring, and active part in hiring engineers.
Our tech context (greenfield)
- Prefer boring tech where it matters; innovate where it differentiates.
- Current stack (continually evolving): TypeScript end-to-end, Next.js (React) with server-side APIs, PostgreSQL, Vercel, …
- Most hard problems sit behind the API: data modelling for tax logic, third-party integrations, and AI pipelines.
- Tooling budget for whatever makes you fast. No legacy, no yak-shaving.
What you’ll bring
- 7+ years building and operating web applications in production, end-to-end
- Strong TypeScript/Node fundamentals; React on the frontend (Next.js or comparable)
- Backend depth: API design, relational data modelling (PostgreSQL), third-party integrations
- A track record of owning features from idea to production with real users
- Product mindset: you talk to users, challenge requirements and cut scope to ship
- AI deeply integrated into your daily engineering work – beyond autocompletion
- Experience with containerised deployments (e.g. Docker) and CI/CD
- A degree in computer science or a related field – or an equivalent track record
- Very good English (min. C1); German is a plus
- A valid work permit for Germany and readiness to work on-site in Munich
Nice to have
- LLM-based features in production (RAG, agents, evaluations)
- Experience in a start-up or scale-up
- Exposure to fintech, accounting, tax or payroll
The process (fast, meaningful, no homework)
- Intro call with a recruiter
- Get to know a fellow engineer, virtually or in person (up to you)
- Meet our founding team in a final interview (in person, ~3h, incl. live coding)
