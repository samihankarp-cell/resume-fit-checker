Staff Software Engineer at Skalar

The opportunity
How taxation, accounting and payroll work today is a pain for everyone. Still every business must do it continuously. It is the backbone our economy runs on.
Enabled through AI, we are turning this often-painful process into a radically simple experience for our customers, so they can focus on what matters to them: growing their business.
The role
As a Staff Software Engineer, you are the technical counterpart to our founders. You look beyond a single product area: you shape the architecture across the platform, make the decisions that are expensive to reverse, and make sure we scale from one country to many without rewriting the core.
You stay hands-on and write a lot of code – but your impact is measured by what the whole engineering team ships.
What you’ll own
- Technical direction: Define architecture and tech strategy across the platform; write and drive RFCs for the decisions that matter.
- Core systems: Own the foundations every product area builds on – the domain model for tax and accounting logic and the integration layer to third-party systems.
- AI correctness at scale: Set the standards for how we build, evaluate and monitor AI pipelines that have to be correct, not just plausible.
- Scale and reliability: Prepare the platform for more countries, more customers and higher compliance demands – security, auditability, observability.
- Engineering bar: Shape how we build – review standards, tooling, delivery practices; mentor senior engineers and own the technical hiring bar.
- Partner to the founders: Translate business strategy into a technical roadmap and spot where technology creates leverage.
Our tech context (greenfield)
Prefer boring tech where it matters; innovate where it differentiates.
Current stack (continually evolving): TypeScript end-to-end, Next.js (React) with server-side APIs, PostgreSQL, Vercel, …
Most hard problems sit behind the API: data modelling for tax logic, third-party integrations, and AI pipelines.
You will decide how this stack evolves as we scale.
What you’ll bring
- 8+ years building and operating production systems, including several years in a technical leadership role (Staff, Lead, Principal or equivalent)
- A track record of designing and evolving the architecture of a product that scaled – in users, data and team size
- Deep backend expertise: data modelling, API design, distributed systems, integrations; strong TypeScript/Node (or the depth to get there fast)
- Influence without authority: you have led technical decisions across team boundaries, with clear written design docs
- Experience making product and business trade-offs together with founders or senior leadership
- You have mentored senior engineers and shaped hiring
- AI deeply integrated into your daily engineering work, plus experience shipping LLM-based features to production
- A degree in computer science or a related field – or an equivalent track record
- Very good English (min. C1); German is a plus
- A valid work permit for Germany and readiness to work on-site in Munich
Nice to have
- Early engineer at a start-up that scaled successfully
- Systems in regulated domains (fintech, accounting, tax, payroll, insurance)
- Multi-country or multi-jurisdiction platforms
- LLM evaluation, RAG or agentic workflows at production scale
What success looks like after 12 months
- Our architecture is ready for the next country without a rewrite.
- Engineers across product areas ship faster because of the foundations and standards you set.
- You are the founders’ go-to partner for every major technical decision.
What you’ll get
- A seat at the table for product and company strategy
- A senior IC path: grow towards Principal – or into engineering leadership, your choice
- The perfect environment to build relevant skills in a world of AI
- Above-market compensation + an equity package with real upside
The process (fast, meaningful, no homework)
- Intro call with a recruiter
- Technical deep-dive with a fellow engineer: walk us through a system you designed (virtually or in person)
- Meet our founding team in a final interview (in person, ~3h, incl. system design and live coding)
