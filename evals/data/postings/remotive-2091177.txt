Content Reviewer - United States at TELUS Digital

Every search represents someone seeking reliable information. They depend on search results being accurate, trustworthy, and relevant to their local context. But search engines only learn to return trustworthy information if human evaluators with deep research expertise and cultural knowledge verify and correct them. Your linguistic precision, fact-checking rigor, and understanding of how your culture communicates and values information is irreplaceable in building search systems that people can actually trust.

In this role, you will evaluate search queries, results, and AI-generated content for factual accuracy, authority, relevance, and user intent alignment. Your judgment determines whether search systems return trustworthy, contextually appropriate information.

- Analyze multi-turn search queries and web results for factual accuracy, source authority, and user intent alignment

- Conduct cross-referencing and fact-checking using regional sources, specialized databases, academic resources, and web directories

- Assess localized content relevancy across diverse domains—news, sports, local culture, technical queries considering regional context and cultural nuance

- Evaluate AI-generated content for factual grounding, hallucination risk, and contextual appropriateness

- Provide structured assessments that train search ranking and content relevance models
