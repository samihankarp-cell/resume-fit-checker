About The Role
IT Operations keeps the company's core productivity, identity, and device ecosystem running smoothly and securely. As IT Systems Automation Engineer, you will own the design, implementation, and maintenance of automation across our corporate IT platforms (including M365, Google Workspace, JAMF, Microsoft Intune, Atlassian & Slack). You'll reduce manual work for the team, improve consistency and security across the employee lifecycle. You’ll help build the scripts, integrations, and workflows that let a 6-person team support the whole organization efficiently. This is a hands-on engineering role within IT Operations, not a helpdesk position - you'll spend most of your time building and improving systems rather than handling day-to-day tickets.

What You’ll Do
- Automate the employee lifecycle: Design and maintain automated workflows for onboarding, offboarding, and role changes across M365, Google Workspace, JAMF, Intune, and Slack (provisioning/deprovisioning accounts, licenses, group memberships, and device enrollment)

- Device management: Build and maintain JAMF (macOS/iOS) and Intune (Windows/mobile) configuration profiles, compliance policies, and automated patch/update workflows.

- Identity & access automation: Automate group and license assignment, conditional access rules, and access reviews across M365 and Google Workspace, working closely with the security team where relevant.

- Collaboration tooling: Manage and automate Slack workspace administration - channel provisioning, app/integration approvals, user lifecycle, and usage policies.

- Scripting & integration: Write and maintain scripts (PowerShell, Google Apps Script, or similar) and use APIs to connect systems that don't natively integrate.

- Documentation & runbooks: Document automation logic, workflows, and recovery procedures so the rest of the team can support and extend what you build.

- Monitoring & reliability: Set up monitoring/alerting for automated workflows so failures (e.g., a broken onboarding script) are caught and resolved quickly.

- Continuous improvement: Identify manual, repetitive IT Operations tasks and propose/build automation to eliminate them.

- Vendor & tooling evaluation: Assess new automation tools or platform features (e.g., new Intune or JAMF capabilities) and recommend adoption where it adds value.

- Agentic support: Design, build, and operate AI agents that resolve common IT support requests end-to-end (e.g., password resets, access and software requests, device troubleshooting) via Slack or the service desk, connecting them to M365, Google Workspace, JAMF, and Intune through APIs, with clear guardrails, approval steps, human escalation paths, and audit logging.

- AI enablement: Roll out and administer AI tools across the organization (e.g., Microsoft Copilot, Claude AI/Code, or similar), including license assignment, data access and governance settings, and usage policies, working closely with security, legal, and the works council where relevant, and support employees with onboarding guides, training materials, and best practices.

What We’re Looking For
- Required:
• Experience in IT Operations, Systems Administration, or a similar role with hands-on scripting/automation responsibility.
• Practical experience administering at least two of: Microsoft 365 (Entra ID/Exchange/SharePoint admin), Google Workspace, JAMF, Microsoft Intune.
• Working proficiency in at least one scripting/automation language (PowerShell, Python, Bash, or Google Apps Script) and comfort using REST APIs.
• Understanding of core identity and device management concepts: SSO, MDM/UEM, conditional access, device compliance.
• Ability to document processes clearly for a small team that needs to maintain what you build.

- Nice to Have:
• Experience with Slack administration or Slack API/workflow automation.
• Experience with a scripting/orchestration platform (e.g., Workato, Tines, Zapier, Azure Automation, or similar iPaaS tools).
• Relevant certifications (e.g., Microsoft MS-102/MD-102, Jamf Certified Admin, Google Workspace Administrator).
• Familiarity with security/compliance frameworks relevant to endpoint and identity management (e.g., SOC 2, ISO 27001).
• Experience working on a small IT team where you own end-to-end delivery rather than a narrow slice of a larger org.
