The Role
As an independent agent, you take inbound calls for ShyftOff client programs and help customers answer questions, resolve issues, and get what they need. After you join, you choose which programs to apply for based on your qualifications and availability.
One of our newest programs include:
Financial Services Program — $15–$20 per hour plus incentives + $300 certification bonus
Help cardholders manage their money with confidence. You'll take inbound calls from people who rely on their banking cards for everyday life: activating a new card, replacing a lost one, sorting out a disputed charge, or getting a time-sensitive payment through. This is credentialed financial services work on an established account, built for people who want to meet a real bar and stick around.
What you'll do:
- Handle inbound calls on card activation, replacements, lost or stolen cards, balances, and account questions
- Help customers with payments, transfers, and disputes, accurately and securely
- Verify customer identity and follow strict security and compliance protocols on every call
- Spot and help resolve potential fraud with composure
- Document every interaction accurately in the account's systems
Who you'll talk to: cardholders and their benefits managers, often at a stressful moment like a lost card or a charge they don't recognize. Your job is to guide them, protect their account, and get them back on solid ground.
Healthcare Program — $14–$20 per hour plus incentives + $200 certification bonus
