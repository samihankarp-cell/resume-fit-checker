CEO Office Associate at Parloa

About Parloa
Parloa's mission is to make every customer conversation feel effortless for both customers and the companies serving them. As agentic AI accelerates, Parloans are shaping the foundation of a new era in customer experience, one where customer support is no longer transactions, but meaningful exchanges. It is not just a vision; Parloa has powered over ONE BILLION interactions between global enterprise brands and their customers, with companies like Booking.com,  HealthEquity, Allianz, SAP, BarmeniaGothaer, and TUI already deploying Parloa at scale.
About the role:
As a CEO Office Associate, you'll take on strategic problems that don't yet have a home and turn them into tangible outcomes, fast. From day one, you'll own complex, cross-functional projects end-to-end, stepping in wherever the business needs leverage most.
Areas of ownership:
- Special Projects & Strategic Programs: Own CEO-sponsored programs that cut across the org end-to-end. You identify something that’s broken, diagnose the problem, build the plan, drive execution across functions, and own the outcome end-to-end.

- Internal Communications: Own the company's heartbeat. Run the monthly All-Hands end-to-end (format, content, narrative, delivery), as well as other company-critical communication events, turning leadership intent into messages that land.

- CEO Office Automations: You are constantly at the forefront of AI development & always aim to increase your own as well as the CEO Office’s leverage. You help the team rethink how it works, every single week.

Who you are:
- Experience: 3+ years in a high-intensity environment (strategy consulting, VC/PE, or a high-growth startup) where you've owned outcomes, not just tasks. 

- Academic Excellence: Strong academic credentials or an equivalent track record of exceptional achievement.

- High IQ, Low Ego: High emotional intelligence, sound judgment, and the humility to step in wherever needed to help the team succeed.

- Executive Presence: Strong communicator and storyteller who builds executive-grade decks, documents, and narratives that both convince and inspire. You’re comfortable interacting with senior internal & external stakeholders.

- Builder Mentality: You don’t just strategize - you execute. You thrive in ambiguity, make the call when there's no playbook, and own the result. 

- First-Principles Thinker: You don't inherit assumptions; you question why things exist before you rebuild them.

- AI-native: You leverage AI tools and automation to work more effectively. Experience with platforms such as n8n is a plus.

- Location: Able to work from our Berlin headquarters five days per week.
