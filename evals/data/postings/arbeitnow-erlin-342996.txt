Data Scientist (m/f/x) at Diamontech AG

Your mission

- Experience in the MedTech sector is a plus
- Solid academic background: PhD or MSc in Physics, Biology or related discipline
- Proficiency in Python: writing clean, tested, version-controlled code; working knowledge of Bash and Docker
- Already living in Berlin or willing to relocate here, already holding a valid EU work permit
- Strong communication skills in English, both written and spoken
